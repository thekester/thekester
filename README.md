# Théophile Avenel

**Software Engineer · Independent Founder · Former Visiting Researcher**

I am a France-based software engineer working at the intersection of software development, networks, telecommunications, and applied research. After three years as a network software engineering apprentice at Orange, I started my own independent software engineering business, **Tavenel**, to build practical solutions for organizations.

My work focuses on Python development, backend systems, APIs, automation, and software infrastructure. I am also interested in cybersecurity, open-source intelligence, and understanding how systems work beyond their intended interfaces.

Previously, I was a **Visiting Researcher at the University of Strathclyde** in Scotland, where I contributed to research involving artificial intelligence and coastal erosion. That experience strengthened my interest in connecting scientific exploration with software engineering.

## Looking toward 2030

My ambition is to continue learning through advanced study at leading academic institutions while building and contributing to ambitious software and research projects. I want to explore new ideas, collaborate across disciplines, and turn technically challenging problems into useful, real-world systems.
